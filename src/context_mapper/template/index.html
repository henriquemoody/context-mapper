How to read

An arrow from A to B means A depends on B. Upstream is at the top. The outer boxes are domains, the coloured boxes inside them are bounded contexts. The Show & hide form in the right panel has a control for every domain and cluster: a multi-context domain or a cluster can be expanded (the boxes inside it), collapsed (one box), or hidden — a dashed underline marks a cluster's — and a one-box domain hides with a toggle. The Aggregates level draws each bounded context around its aggregates, and an arrow between two aggregates is a reference. Focusing on a domain or a cluster lists what it holds, and each item there can be hidden too. Click a box to focus on it. Download saves the diagram you are looking at as a PNG or SVG image.
